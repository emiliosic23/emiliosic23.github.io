I work on financial markets and the macroeconomy — how information, frictions and investors' perceptions of risk shape asset prices.

About

I am a first-year PhD student in Economics and Finance at Bocconi University, on the Finance curriculum. I hold a BSc in Economics and Finance (2022) and an MSc in Economic and Social Sciences (2025), both from Bocconi.

After graduating I worked for a year and a half at the European Central Bank on financial markets and financial stability, and then in the private sector as a macro strategist.

My research interests are financial economics, financial markets and the macroeconomy, asset pricing, behavioural finance, informational frictions and monetary policy.
